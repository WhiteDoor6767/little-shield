Our Story

About Little Shield

We are a community-driven NGO working to protect children in urban slum settlements, building safe spaces where every child can learn and thrive.

Who We Are

Guardians of Childhood

Little Shield was born from a simple but urgent truth: children in India's urban slum communities are among the most vulnerable — yet the most overlooked. Surrounded by poverty, lack of education, and systemic neglect, these children face risks that no child should ever know.

We are a team of passionate community workers, educators, advocates, and local volunteers committed to changing that reality. We work at the grassroots level — going door to door, partnering with local police, running awareness workshops, and building safe spaces where children can simply be children.

Our Core Values

❤️

Child-First Always

Every decision, every program, every partnership is evaluated on one metric: what is best for the child.

We don't work for communities — we work with them. Real change only happens when communities lead it.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

Every milestone is a testament to what happens when communities choose to protect their most vulnerable members.

2023
Little Shield Founded
A small group of volunteers began running awareness sessions in 2 slum communities in the city.
2024
Police Partnership Launched
Formalized child-friendly response protocols with local police units across 5 communities.
2024
Education Access Program
Helped enroll 100+ children in schools and tutoring programs who had previously dropped out.
2025
Expanded to 12 Communities
Scaled operations across 12 slum settlements, reaching 500+ families with our programs.
2026
Mission 2026 — Full Awareness Drive
Launching our most ambitious campaign yet: city-wide awareness, door-to-door drives, and education for all.
Our Vision
◦ ◦ ◦

A City Where No Child Is Left Behind

We envision urban communities where every child — regardless of which slum they grew up in — has access to safety, education, and the chance to fulfil their potential.

Our mission is to build a movement of informed, empowered communities that collectively refuse to let any child be abused, exploited, or denied their future.
