Mission 2026

Our Goals for Every Child

To spread awareness about child rights and safety in slum communities, and ensure every child has access to quality education by 2026.

What We're Working Toward

Our Three Core Goals in 2026

01
Awareness

Reach Every Family

Run door-to-door drives, street plays, and community workshops to ensure every family in our 12 communities understands child rights, warning signs of abuse, and how to get help.

Progress65%
02
Education

Education for Every Child

Ensure 100% school enrollment among children in our partner communities, backed by tutoring programs, life-skills training, and free learning materials.

Progress48%
03
Safety Network

Build a Safety Network

Establish a robust network connecting communities, local police, Child Welfare Committees, and our volunteers for rapid, child-friendly response to any safety concern.

Progress72%
Key Focus Areas for 2026

Five Pillars of Change

Together, these focus areas build a comprehensive safety net for every child in our communities.

👁️

Awareness Campaigns

Community media outreach and campaigns on child rights and safety.

🚔

Police Partnership

Work with local police for quick, child-friendly responses to cases.

🎓

Education Access

School enrollment, tutoring, and life-skills training for all children.

👪

Parent Sensitization

Workshops and street plays engaging parents and community elders.

🚪

Door-to-Door Drives

Home visits to directly inform families about child rights and protection.
