What We Do

Our Programs & Initiatives

Five community-driven programs working together to protect children, build awareness, and ensure every child can access a safe and educated future.

All Programs

Program 01

Community Awareness

Our awareness campaigns are the foundation of everything we do. Through media outreach, street plays, and community events, we bring child rights education directly to the people who need it most.

  • Interactive street theatre performances in slum communities
  • Printed materials in local languages distributed door to door
  • Community radio spots and social media awareness campaigns
  • Workshops for teachers, community leaders, and youth groups
  • Annual child rights awareness fairs

Program 02

Police Partnership

We work closely with local law enforcement to create child-friendly response systems — ensuring that when a child is in danger, help is immediate, sensitive, and effective.

  • Training local police in child-friendly investigation procedures
  • Dedicated liaison between communities and police units
  • Fast-track reporting systems for child safety cases
  • Joint community awareness programs with police involvement
  • Support for families navigating the legal reporting process

Program 03

Education Access

Education is the most powerful tool against exploitation. We connect children with schools, tutoring programs, and life-skills training — and we support families to keep children in school.

  • School enrollment drives and follow-up support
  • After-school tutoring centres in slum communities
  • Free learning materials, uniforms, and stationery
  • Life-skills workshops: confidence, communication, safety
  • Scholarship support for outstanding children

Program 04

Parent & Community Sensitization

Parents and community elders are among the most important protectors of children. We run targeted programs to sensitize them about child abuse, its signs, how to respond, and the resources available.

  • Monthly parent meetings and workshops
  • Community elder engagement programs
  • Street plays targeting common myths and stigmas around abuse
  • One-on-one counselling support for families
  • Safe parenting guides in local languages

Program 05

Door-to-Door Awareness Drives

We go directly to families — because we know not everyone can come to us. Our trained volunteers visit homes across slum communities to share information on child rights, safety, and available support.

  • Systematic home visits across all partner communities
  • Distribution of child rights information booklets
  • Collection of feedback and concerns from families
  • Connecting families to government schemes and services
  • Identifying at-risk children for follow-up support
